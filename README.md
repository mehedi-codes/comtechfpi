# Computer Technology 5th Semester Archive

## Faridpur Polytechnic Institute

This repository was created to collect and organize the study materials, class notes, video links, and files from my 5th semester Computer Technology classes during the COVID-19 pandemic period.

It reflects a special time in my diploma journey when online learning became the primary mode of education. During that period, I gathered important learning resources, class recordings, lecture links, and study materials to stay connected with my academic subjects and continue my learning from home.

## Overview

This repository is a personal archive of my 5th semester study materials in the Computer Technology department at Faridpur Polytechnic Institute. It includes:

- class notes and subject-related materials
- online video links and learning resources
- practical files and study documents
- assignment and project references
- important academic materials from online classes

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to:

- keep all 5th semester learning materials in one place
- preserve academic resources from online classes during COVID-19
- organize notes and study references for future review
- document my diploma learning journey during a difficult but important academic period

## Academic Context

During my 5th semester, I was studying in the Computer Technology department while attending classes online due to the COVID-19 situation. This period required adaptation to remote learning, digital study habits, and self-driven preparation. This repository captures that experience and the academic work completed during that time.
